OVERALL COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS
======================================================================

Common Factors Observed
-----------------------
1. All three individuals experience loneliness and emotional stress.
2. Academic pressure is a major contributor to emotional struggles.
3. Family and friends act as the primary support systems.
4. All three want to completely overcome loneliness and depression.
5. Mental health challenges affect people regardless of personality type.

Key Differences
----------------
INTROVERT:
- Prefers internal processing of emotions.
- Uses creative or solitary coping methods.
- Less expressive emotionally.

EXTROVERT:
- Depends heavily on social interaction.
- Shares emotions openly.
- Feels affected strongly by social exclusion.

AMBIVERT:
- Maintains balance between social support and personal reflection.
- Flexible and adaptable emotionally.
- Can function independently and socially.

Researcher’s Final Observation
------------------------------
This analysis shows that loneliness and emotional struggles are not limited to one personality type.
Introverts, extroverts, and ambiverts all face mental health issues in different ways.
The main difference lies in how they process emotions and seek support.

- Introverts tend to internalize emotions.
- Extroverts externalize emotions through communication.
- Ambiverts combine both approaches depending on the situation.

The study also highlights the importance of emotional support systems, healthy coping strategies, and understanding mental health among teenagers and students.
